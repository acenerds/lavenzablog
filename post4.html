On... Love

4/27/26

Peer of Gods to me is the man thy presence
Crowns with joy; who hears, as he sits beside thee,
Accents sweet of thy lips the silence breaking,
With lovely laughter;

Tones that make the heart in my bosom flutter,
For if I, the space of a moment even,
Near to thee come, any word I would utter
Instantly fails me;

Vain my stricken tongue would a whisper fashion,
Subtly under my skin runs fire ecstatic;
Straightway mists surge dim to my eyes and leave them
Reft of their vision;

Echoes ring in my ears; a trembling seizes
All my body bathed in soft perspiration;
Pale as grass I grow in my passion's madness,
Like one insensate;

But must I dare all, since to me unworthy,
Bliss thy beauty brings that a God might envy;
Never yet was fervid woman a fairer
Image of Kypris.

Ah! undying Daughter of God, befriend me!
Calm my blood that thrills with impending transport;
Feed my lips the murmur of words to stir her
Bosom to pity;

Overcome with kisses her faintest protest,
Melt her mood to mine with amorous couches,—
Till her low assent and her sigh's abandon
Lure me to rapture.

"Decreation", a term coined by French Philosopher Simone Weil to describe a love that lacks the self. More specifically, it is a process by which one "unmakes" oneself, leaving behind a void that God may fill with Himself. It is the kind of love one might harbour for something divine, something immeasurably greater than oneself.

"Ecstasy", describes an overwhelming experience of emotion, usually joy or pleasure though not exclusively. It stems from the Greek word "ékstasis" meaning "to stand outside oneself" or "to be displaced from oneself" like an out of body experience.

I think this is what it means to love. To love is to stand outside oneself, to gradually erase oneself until all that remains is one's love, while the self is forgotten. I know that sounds melancholy, but it follows from another thought I've always held: that unrequited love is the purest form of love.

In the Bible, it says if you love only those who love you, it really isn't all that special. Even evil people tend to do that much. Whether or not one agree, I understand the sentiment. Loving those who already love you is natural. To love those who will never return your affection is something else entirely. To care for those who could not care less for you feels almost holy. To do for others what they would never do for you feels consecrated.

Obviously, it'd be nice to be loved back, but I'd honestly feel guilty.

I've always wondered what love feels like for an ordinary person. If I were conventionally attractive and socially adept, would I ever have thought this deeply about love at all? Or would I just see someone I like and go win without a second thought? How do normal people experience love? Because this is the only way I can experience love, because it's my natural instinct to disappear into the empty space surrounding another person, I have trouble imagining what a healthier or more "normal" kind of love must feel like. If someone loved me in the way I want to love other's I'd honestly probably worry about them.

I know it's hypocritical, I know that okay? I'm willing to accept that kind of hurt for myself and would rather no one concern themself with me, yet I wouldn't enjoy seeing another person feel the same way about themself. I would want them to value themselves more than I seem capable of valuing myself. Like Black Leg Sanji!

As established, I don't know how to end these "On..." pieces. They aren't arguments or conclusions so much as thoughts, random, random thoughts.
