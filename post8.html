A Recreation of a Recreation

8/19/26

Recently, a girl from my school sent me a voice message. I've always always always wondered how a normal person's brain works. This is not something worth noting, not to her, not to any sane people, just to me (and maybe you). It's become a problem. I've listened to it a million times, I can recite it from memory.

"Hey Lavenza... yeah so I've been in Botswana for about... three weeks now? I guess if it's uh... it's a Wednesday night then yeah, exactly three weeks. And... yeah. It's been- it's been interesting, it's been good! Anyway... how are you doing? Let me know. The thing is I'm a little bit drunk right now so that's why I was like 'I'm not gonna text Lavenza,' But! Let me know how you're doing, how are thingssss... yeah. Bye bye."

It is charming, it is sweet and earnest. It's not romantic. It's just normal. I like the pauses she leaves where you can tell she's still thinking. The way she talks when she's drunk compared to when she's sober isn't actually that different. I've gone back to this message repeatedly, like a dog scratching the same itch nonstop. Soon they're gonna have to give me a cone so that I won't itch it anymore. I can't help but feel like a creep for this, and you see why, don't you? I'm sure you're judging me too.

In like sad movies sometimes the protagonist can't get over a dead loved one so he usually plays her last voicemail over and over or something while leaning against a wall all emo-like. Frankie Muniz did that in an episode of a TV show I saw, it wasn't Malcolm in the Middle. I'm pretty sure he played a killer in the show I'm thinking of. I'm not a sad emo movie protagonist, nor am I a serial killer, nor am I Franki Muniz. The girl who sent me this voice message isn't even dead! She's just studying abroad until the second quarter of school. But this is just sort of this tendency I have, I think it's innate to being a writer (and a LOSER). It's to be able to rewrite and romanticize everything that happens in my life.

Writing is a cracked, imperfect vessel in which you pour foggy, hazy, imperfect memories. God I am so pretentious. But if you can't be pretentious in college, where can you be? Storytelling in general is the pervert's dream. At its core, writing is just observing the world around you. But that'd be boring. There is air going into my lungs, there is air leaving my lungs. There are fingers typing on a keyboard. There are words on the screen. I am not a Buddhist Monk, mindfulness is not for me. That's why you have to lie, the objective truth is not particularly interesting.

Anything worth catching and putting into words can't be. Therefore all you can do is make your hollow recreation. You can pin her down between pages, ink and haze clouding the depiction of her. Ceci n'est pas une pipe. Ceci n'est pas une fille mignonne. I google translated that, I don't speak French.

I realize I sound insane and creepy but the point I'm trying to make is actually pretty simple: what you write isn't the actual person, you could never write the actual person, you can only write the recreation that exists in your head. You're recreating a recreation of a real person. There's degrees of separation. Kevin Bacon or whatever I don't know. Romanticization exists somewhere in those degrees. You know those people who think EVERYONE is flirting with them? I imagine this is how their brain works, it only operates within those degrees. First the real happens, someone opens the door for them or tells them to have a nice day. Then, they think it over in their head and already start morphing and shaping it differently. Finally, they go tell their friend "Oh my gosh you'll never believe what happened" and set their recreation of a recreation of a person free. Storytelling is the pervert's dream.

But that's not what I'm doing with her voice message. At least not exactly. The problem in the voice message is that it is very hard to romanticize. Not because it's not cute or not whatever, but because it is too perfect. It isn't a hazy memory I can write about. I can't misremember a word or cast a glint in her eyes that wasn't there. Listening to it on loop, I know for a fact I can't hear any sort of romantic affection in her voice. This message has me trapped. It is checkmate. It won't allow me to make my recreation of a recreation of a person. It IS the person still. A perfect memory is antithetical to writing, to crafting the story you want instead of the one that's actually there. A recording has the same effect.

This recording is my punishment for being such a creep that I find love and affection where there is none. I wrote an essay about this, the word "yearning" and how technically if we go off the root of the word it's actually a sin. I'm a sinner. Samael will be here soon to levy the accusations and judgements against me. They're gonna rip my pneuma out thus preventing me from obtaining gnosis and leaving me trapped in the Demiurge's damned world forever. I only know those terms cuz of JRPGs I'm not well read at all. I say this a lot, but I really don't understand how normal people feel. I know this shame I feel when I like someone is not normal. People get embarrassed, yes, shy and flustered, when they've got a "crush." But they don't tend to feel "guilty" do they? To me, it feels too intrusive even just to have those feelings at all. Like I’m infringing on their existence with my feelings. I'm sure that's something that should be unpacked in therapy or at a catholic church maybe but I'm all out of both. I don’t want this to come across as TOO self-loating-y or anything, I think I just tend to worry.

I don't want this to end on "Wah wah wah I'm a whiny baby who thinks creepy thoughts and is a LOSER" (even though that's 100% true) since I imagine it'd be miserable to read. I think if I must have conclusion (which I really don't want to have, I've always hated the idea that real life is a "story" with "messages" and "lessons" and conclusions as if things don't just happen for no reason) it should be that those degrees are scary and dangerous and also awesome. I'm an English major, I can't exactly be against recreating recreations of real things. That'd kind of kill my whole gimmick. I think you just have to be cautious. More often than not the real thing and the recreation don't line up, I'd go so far as to say they never line up. And that's alright, so long as you don't get obsessive. If you NEED the White Whale to represent something divine and grandiose and evil and be a pasteboard mask for all that is hidden about the universe, you'll be strangled by your harpoon rope and never reach your end goal and also your boat sinks and only a depressed gay guy survives inside a coffin. The real and ideal are separate, they don't have to be complete opposites, but they have to be kept apart at all costs regardless of how well or badly they line up. Like how D4C works in Jojo Part 7.
