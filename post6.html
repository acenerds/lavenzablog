Music at the Library

4/28/26

They're playing music at the library right now. I have no clue why. I'm not being poetic or anything, there's a band in the area where I usually like to sit (the chairs are so soft and comfortable, as opposed to the hard wooden chairs in the rest of the library) and they're performing. I don't want to be a downer, I try to be positive, but is that not insanely annoying?

The library is supposed to be quiet, you can have sleepy sort of ambient music but you can't be playing drums and sax dude, that's just not right. Music is awesome, I love Laufey and Johnny Cash and The Cardigans. I mean, come on, music is universally beloved, isn't it? Is there anyone, aside from maybe a couple deaf people, who dislike music? Music is for falling in love and grieving and reliving childhood!

But I just want some peace and quiet, that's what a library is for, isn't it? I guess even something as loved as music can be exhausting in the right context.

It's been three months since I've written an update, and I don't really have any reason except laziness. In these three months... I'm kind of sad to say nothing particularly interesting nor eventful happened. I moved out of my college dorm and in with my sisters, I've not been able to find a job and we're really really poor so I feel like a burden most of the time, and that's really it.

I really really really don't want these blogs to be nothing but whining. I don't. I want to be a self sufficient hero who can take care of themself. I want to live in a rundown apartment, eat one meal a day, have a dead end job, and overall live a simple and bummy life. I've always thought it'd be fun to be a monk or something, but I'd hate to shave my head. I'd get to live up in the mountains, spend all day praying and meditating, and eat simple and humble meals every day.

Of course, I'm really not cut out for that sort of life. I'm way too worldly. Sure we can say that ultimately things don't mean or matter and only exist if we let them, but unfortunately, I wanna give things meaning and matter so I can play rpg and eat yummy snacks. I wanna listen to music (when it isn't being played at the library). I can detach from many things, but definetely not everything they'd demand of me. I don't really care for things like clothes and personal relationships and most material possessions, but some things just can't be released no matter what.

Living with my sisters is pretty awful. It makes me wonder when I'll feel comfortable in a home. During the school year I had been counting down the days when I'd finally "get to" move out, and now I'm counting down the reverse. I WANT to live in a dorm room again. You know how crazy that is? There's still 57 days left (as of writing this). I don't want to make it seem like I hate my sisters or anything, I just don't know if we're cut out for living together. The oldest one is the only real issue to be honest.

She's always either drunk or high and it's so frustrating to interact with her when she's like that. I don't have anything against alchohol or weed or anything stupid like that, I fucking love beer and cigarettes. But it's constant with her, I only do that stuff at parties! When she's like that, she's so sensitive she'll freak out over anything and she'll never leave me alone and she'll always either lecture me or shout at me or anything over nothing! That's why I've been going to the library every day, I don't want to be home when she's home.

It's so frustrating because she's a teacher, which means she's home during summer just like I'm home during summer. I don't even have solace in her going to work!! She's constantly guilt-tripping me about how I don't pay rent or for groceries or anything as if I don't feel terrible and am not constantly looking for jobs! I'm doing what a can! But there's no real way to defend myself without sounding like a total bum, which I am. It's unfortunate that you can't really defend yourself without coming across as... defensive. I'm just kind of doing my best.

Just last night for some reason she was going off on me while high and drunk again. She bought a remote but we don't have any batteries for it. She called me into the living room to tell me that it needs AAA batteries. I nodded and said "Yeah" and somehow that was enough to get her to start shouting at me? She kept trying to correct me and say "No AA batteries won't fit!" but I never said anything about AA batteries! I just walked off. And another time, I came back from someones house doing yardwork for money and she started yelling at me about how she pays for everything like usual! She wouldn't listen to me explaining that I was planning on giving her the money and just kept prattling on and on.

They stopped playing music at the library. The band finally shut up. They packed up their drums and dragged away the mic stands and the library became a library again. Why would they even have that? Isn't that insane? I didn't even really notice right away when the music stopped. Probably because I had been tuning it out, drowning out the music with the noise in my head and on my keyboard. Even though the music is gone, that noise still remains unfortunately. But that's really what being a human means. It's never really quiet, is it? Or at least not for me.

I'm hoping to get out of the house more, but I don't really know where else to go besides the library.
